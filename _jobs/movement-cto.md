**Host:** Public AI Network  
**Location:** Remote  
**Commitment:** Part-time, volunteer  
**Compensation:** Unpaid

As the Movement CTO for the public AI movement, you will be responsible for architecting and developing a robust, flexible, and modular cross-organizational technical infrastructure including a contributor/customer management system (CMS), a communication system (currently Slack), knowledge sharing infrastructure (currently Google Drive), events calendars, forums, GitHub, CI/CD pipelines, voting / public feedback systems, various websites that can be deployed throughout the decentralized and global public AI movement, and eventually AI models deployed either in-house, through affiliated governments, or on open source hubs.

## Qualifications

- Strong technical skills, including prior experience leading a technical team.
- Familiarity with the technology needs and patterns of social movements, remote and distributed organizations, and/or political campaigns.
