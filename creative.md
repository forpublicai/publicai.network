## About the Fellowship

The **Public AI Creative Fellows** project explores how artists, designers, and creative technologists can help shape the public conversation around artificial intelligence. While much of the discourse around AI is driven by technical or policy communities, this fellowship seeks to broaden participation by supporting creative experimentation and cultural critique.

Creative practitioners can contribute to the public AI movement by shaping narratives, experimenting with new formats, and reaching audiences outside the typical AI policy or technical communities. Through interactive projects, experimental websites, and cultural artifacts, the fellows explore social implications of AI including labor, governance, cultural production, and the future of creative industries.

The fellowship ran from 2025 to early 2026 as part of the [Public AI Network](https://publicai.network), supported by operational partners including [Metagov](https://metagov.org) and [Aspen Digital](https://www.aspendigital.org/).

## Projects

Over the course of the fellowship, the fellows produced a series of experimental projects exploring the future of AI through cultural, economic, and governance lenses.

<div class="project-grid">
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="https://adhdai.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ADHD AI</a></h3>
    <p>A satirical interactive project presenting a fictional AI-powered advertising agency that generates proactive apology campaigns for companies before scandals occur. The piece humorously critiques corporate reputation management, algorithmic PR strategies, and the ways AI could be used to automate damage control.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="https://willihaveajob.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Will I Have A Job?</a></h3>
    <p>An interactive tool allowing users to explore how susceptible they may be to AI-driven automation. Users can either upload their CV or select their profession, after which the system provides a speculative analysis of automation risk. The project invites reflection on labor transitions and public narratives about AI and employment.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="http://botidol.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Bot Idol</a></h3>
    <p>A creative experiment exploring AI-generated personalities and the cultural phenomenon of "AI idols." The project examines authorship, identity, and cultural production in AI-mediated environments.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.artificialnouveau.com/ai-perspectives-survey.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AI Perspectives Survey</a></h3>
    <p>A survey project collecting perspectives from artists and creative practitioners about the role of AI in creative work and its potential impact on cultural production.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.artificialnouveau.com/publicai/moreaiineedit/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">More AI I Need It</a></h3>
    <p>A creative exploration of the paradoxical relationship many creative workers have with AI—simultaneously anxious about its impacts while feeling pressure to adopt it in order to remain competitive.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="https://artificialnouveau.github.io/publicai/ai-governance-game/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Public AI Governance Game</a></h3>
    <p>An interactive educational tool designed to help users understand trade-offs in AI governance. The game simulates decision-making around AI development and deployment, balancing priorities such as openness, safety, economic incentives, and public benefit.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="http://doesthisjobexist.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Does This Job Exist?</a></h3>
    <p>A tool and Phantasm chrome extension helping job seekers avoid wasting time applying for ghost jobs that aren't actually hiring. Research shows at least 25% of job postings are ghost posts. The project addresses the philanthropic effort of supporting job seekers in navigating the modern labor market.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="project-card">

## Connection to Public AI Seminar

The fellowship period coincided with **Season 3 of the [Public AI Seminar](https://publicai.network/seminar)**, which focused on the intersection between AI and the creative industries. The seminar explored AI through four lenses:

- Political power
- Cultural power  
- Technological power
- Economic power

Speakers included artists, writers, technologists, and policymakers such as Ted Chiang, Mat Dryhurst, Reema Selhi, Gideon Lichfield, and Kim Stanley Robinson. The seminar served to build intellectual infrastructure for thinking about public AI while connecting creative professionals with policy and governance discussions.

## Key Insights

**Speed of Creative Development:** Using contemporary AI tools and lightweight web publishing infrastructure, it is now possible to move from concept to public-facing artifact very quickly. This enables rapid experimentation with new ideas and narratives about AI.

**Creative Industries as Pressure Points:** The creative industries represent both a pressure point and an opportunity in the broader AI debate. Questions around authorship, training data, automation of creative work, and economic value are already intensely debated within these communities.

**Creative Artifacts as Policy Prototypes:** Interactive websites, narrative experiences, and games can simulate governance trade-offs or social futures in ways that are more accessible than traditional policy reports. Creative artifacts can function as lightweight policy prototypes.

## Related Initiatives

The fellowship was part of a broader ecosystem of creative engagement with public AI, including:

- **[The Protopian Prize](https://www.protopianprize.com)** — A $5,000 prize supporting speculative cultural work about constructive technological futures, judged by Gideon Lichfield (former Editor-in-Chief of _Wired_), Kevin Kelly (founding editor of _Wired_), and science fiction authors.

- **Ongoing partnerships** with organizations working at the intersection of AI, culture, and creative industries, including the Creators Coalition for AI (CCAI) and the Berggruen Institute.
