The Public AI Network Advisory Board brings together leaders across technology, policy, culture, and public infrastructure who advise the coalition as it builds toward public AI.

## Members

**Brian Behlendorf** — Open Source Security Foundation / Linux Foundation  
Open-source infrastructure leader; Apache co-founder and longtime steward of collaborative security and public-interest technology.

**Diane Coyle** — Bennett Institute, University of Cambridge  
Economist and public-policy scholar focused on the value of digital and shared infrastructure for the common good.

**Mat Dryhurst** — Artist  
Artist and researcher exploring AI, cultural production, and how creative communities shape—and are shaped by—machine learning systems.

**David Eaves** — UCL Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose  
Public-sector technology and digital public infrastructure expert; teaches and advises on how governments build shared digital capacity.

**Lawrence Lessig** — Harvard Law School  
Legal scholar and advocate for open culture, democratic integrity, and institutional designs that keep public goods public.

**Kim Stanley Robinson** — Author  
Novelist whose work on climate, governance, and near-future possibility has shaped how many think about public futures for technology.

**Ed Saperia** — London College of Political Technology (Newspeak House)  
Founder and dean of Newspeak House; builds communities of practice at the intersection of technology, politics, and civil society.

**Bruce Schneier** — Harvard Kennedy School  
Security technologist and public-interest voice on cryptography, trust, and the power dynamics of digital systems.

**Divya Siddarth** — Collective Intelligence Project  
Co-founder and executive director of CIP; works on collective intelligence, democratic AI governance, and public-interest technology design.
