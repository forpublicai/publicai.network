Journalism has long served as a cornerstone of democracy, providing trusted information, accountability, and public discourse. We believe journalists can also benefit from community-based AI services and infrastructure designed specifically for their needs.

The Public AI Network, in partnership with the **Frontier Collective**, **Minnesota Public Radio**, the **Center for Journalism and Liberty**, **NYU**, and other leading journalism organizations, is launching an initiative to bring **community-based AI services** to journalists and newsrooms across the United States. This is your opportunity to join a collaborative effort to make AI **more accessible, trustworthy, and useful** for journalism.

## The Journalism AI Initiative

![Journalism AI team collaboration](assets/journalism/team-photo.jpg)

We are building a journalism-focused AI ecosystem that prioritizes accuracy, transparency, and journalistic integrity. This initiative explores whether specialized language models can perform better than commercial models for journalism-specific tasks.

The program will develop:
- A journalism LLM based on existing small language models
- Community-based AI infrastructure for investigative journalism
- Tools for fact-checking, research, and content analysis
- Resources aligned with journalistic ethics and standards

## Core Research Questions

Our initiative investigates:
- Can specialized small language models outperform commercial general-purpose models for journalism tasks?
- How can AI support investigative journalism while maintaining ethical standards?
- What infrastructure is needed for sovereign AI tools in journalism?
- How can newsrooms share resources and capabilities through collaborative platforms?

## Our Approach

### Sovereign AI Tech Stack
We are exploring the concept of a sovereign AI tech stack for journalism — infrastructure that journalists and newsrooms can control, audit, and trust. This includes:
- Open-source models that can be inspected and customized
- Transparent training data and methodology
- Community governance and oversight
- Privacy-preserving architectures

### Journalism Cloud Alliance
The initiative builds on research from the **Journalism Cloud Alliance**, which examines how cloud infrastructure and collaborative platforms can support investigative journalism. This work provides a foundation for understanding the technical and organizational requirements for shared AI infrastructure.

### Small Language Models for Journalism
Rather than relying solely on large commercial models, we are investigating whether smaller, specialized language models trained on journalism-specific data can:
- Provide more accurate and contextually appropriate results
- Operate with greater transparency and explainability
- Reduce costs and dependencies on commercial platforms
- Better align with journalistic values and practices

## Research Foundation

This initiative draws on several key research areas:

**Cloud Infrastructure for Investigative Journalism**
Research on how cloud platforms can enable collaborative investigative work while maintaining security and editorial independence.

**LLM Applications in Journalism** (Aimee Rinehart's work)
Examining how language models can support journalistic workflows, from research and fact-checking to story development and audience engagement.

**Journalism Ethics and AI**
Exploring how AI tools can be designed and deployed in ways that align with journalistic standards for accuracy, fairness, and accountability.

## Why Journalism Needs Public AI

Journalism faces unique challenges that public AI can help address:
- **Access barriers** — Many newsrooms cannot afford expensive commercial AI subscriptions
- **Trust concerns** — Black-box commercial models raise questions about transparency and bias
- **Specialized needs** — General-purpose AI may not understand journalism-specific contexts
- **Independence** — Reliance on commercial platforms can compromise editorial autonomy
- **Collaboration** — Newsrooms need infrastructure for sharing capabilities and resources

By developing public AI infrastructure for journalism, we can:
- Ensure equitable access to AI tools regardless of newsroom size or resources
- Build transparency and accountability into AI systems used for news production
- Create specialized tools that understand journalistic workflows and standards
- Enable collaboration and resource-sharing across the journalism community
- Maintain editorial independence and control over critical infrastructure

## What Is Public AI?

Public AI is AI as public infrastructure, like water, electricity, highways, public parks, and libraries. 
It is **open, accountable, and sustainably maintained**, providing meaningful access to all.
