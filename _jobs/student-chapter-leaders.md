This opportunity is hosted by [Aspen Digital](https://www.aspendigital.org/), a program of the Aspen Institute.

**Location:** Fully remote  
**Commitment:** Part-time  
**Compensation:** $300 honorarium  
**Program Dates:** From mid-February to mid-May 2027
**Application Deadline:** End-of-day anywhere on Earth 23 December 2026

## The Challenge
We need a purposeful entrypoint for rising leaders who want to engage more deeply with questions of the access, accountability, and sustainability of the AI ecosystem.

In the last year, for the first time since the widespread adoption of generative AI, the share of young Americans (18–29) who said that they were more concerned than excited about AI exceeded 50%. At the same time, this demographic is one of the fastest and most consistent adopters of the technology. Students’ futures will be significantly shaped by these technologies. They deserve to influence how they are built, used, and governed.

## Our Approach
Aspen Digital, in partnership with the Public AI Network, is recruiting student leaders to establish public AI chapters at institutions of higher education across the United States and around the world. Public AI Student Chapters will offer a peer network and leadership development opportunities, while equipping students with skills to shape the AI ecosystem to be more accessible, accountable, and sustainable. These chapters will serve as the launching pad for rising policymakers, technologists, and activists who know that another AI is possible.

## Student Leaders Cohort
Public AI Student Leaders is a leadership development program for students who wish to advance the values of public AI—public access, public accountability, and permanent public goods—within their campus communities. Students will participate in a curated cohort of public AI student leaders, grow their knowledge and skills around public AI, build with public AI tools and systems, and meet with technology, activist, and policy leaders from around the world.

## Student Leaders will:

- found and lead Public AI Student Chapters at their college, university, or institution of higher education;
- recruit members from their campus communities;
- run initiatives (events, workshops, lectures, etc.) that advance participation in and development of public AI;
- develop partnerships with other student organizations on campus; and
- collaborate with other chapter leads to create a replicable playbook and resources for other student leaders to start chapters on their campuses.

## Details & Requirements
Participants in the leadership development cohort will receive a $300 honorarium to acknowledge their contributions.

This program is open to students of all backgrounds and areas of study who are 18 years of age or older. The program will be conducted in English, and participants are expected to be comfortable conversing, reading, and writing in English. Application answers must be submitted in English. 

Participants should expect to commit an average of 5 hours per week over the 14 weeks of the program, from mid-February to mid-May 2027, for a total of 70–80 hours over the whole program. Participants accepted into the cohort will need to confirm their participation the first week of February. The cohort programming will begin mid-February.

**[Applications are open](https://www.aspendigital.org/project/public-ai-student-chapters/) through end-of-day, anywhere on Earth, 23 December 2026.**
