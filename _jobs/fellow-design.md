This opportunity is funded by and offered in collaboration with [Current AI](https://currentai.org), a Paris-based nonprofit.

**Location:** Fully remote, with a preference for hubs with strong open-source AI presence (Berlin, Paris, London, San Francisco, NYC, Toronto)  
**Commitment:** Deliverable-based, then part-time  
**Compensation:** €5,000 stipend  
**Start date:** ASAP

We are seeking a dynamic and mission-driven Public AI Fellow to own design for the AI Potluck—the strategy Public AI and Current AI use to convene open-source AI contributors around shared public-interest goals, rather than shilling for any single project.

This is a design-and-product role for a movement, not a startup pitch deck. You'll work on frontend web, application, and product design that makes the potluck legible, welcoming, and useful: shared spaces where dozens of builders, maintainers, and contributors can find each other, understand what's happening, and collaborate without Public AI becoming the protagonist.

You'll work closely with our CTO and the broader potluck team on interfaces, flows, and visual language that hold up across many projects, orgs, and time zones.

This isn't a brand-design job for one product. It's design for a convener.

## You will

- Design frontend web experiences for the AI Potluck—landing pages, program sites, shared workspaces, and other surfaces that help people orient and participate.
- Shape application and product design for potluck-related tools: how contributors discover work, join sessions, share context, and follow through on cross-org collaborations.
- Build a coherent visual and interaction language for Public AI's potluck work—clear, credible, and open enough to host many voices without feeling like corporate DevRel.
- Work with engineering to ship: prototypes, specs, component patterns, and design reviews that unblock implementation rather than pile up in Figma.
- Design for convening, not conversion. The goal is to elevate other people's projects and make coordination easier—not to funnel attention to a single Public AI product.
- Iterate from real use: workshops, working sessions, CRM-adjacent flows, contributor onboarding, and whatever surfaces the potluck strategy needs next.
- Partner with Current AI, our engineering team at [github.com/forpublicai](https://github.com/forpublicai), and peer institutions across the public AI ecosystem.

## You might be a fit if you have

- Strong product and UI design instincts—you can move from messy stakeholder input to clear flows, wireframes, and polished frontend work.
- Frontend fluency: comfortable specifying or implementing web UI (HTML/CSS, a modern component framework, or equivalent). You don't have to be a full-time engineer, but you should speak their language.
- Experience designing for communities, platforms, or multi-stakeholder products—not only single-brand marketing sites or one-app SaaS.
- Taste for public-interest, civic, or open-source contexts: design that feels trustworthy, readable, and slightly under-branded on purpose.
- Working familiarity with the open-source AI landscape (models, inference, tooling, evals) or the curiosity to learn it quickly from the people you're designing with.
- Comfort collaborating asynchronously across organizations and time zones.
- Bonus if you've designed event, program, or fellowship experiences—not just product dashboards.
- Bonus if you've built or contributed to a design system used across multiple products or teams.
- Bonus if you've worked on open-source or public-goods software where the UI had to serve many maintainers, not one PM.

This is an opportunity to define how a growing public-interest AI movement looks and feels online—at a moment when the potluck strategy is moving from idea to infrastructure. You'll work with technologists, organizers, maintainers, and public-interest institutions who care about making open-source AI more than the sum of its repos.
